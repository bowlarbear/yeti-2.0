# Start Here

1. This is a manual Bitcoin Core vault, not a product and not customer support

2. Follow the steps as they are written. Do not improvise. 

3. If you need legal recourse, a regulated custodian, or someone at a company to call, this guide is the wrong tool. Use a brokerage, ETF, or trust. That is a different contract, not Bitcoin self-custody.

4. You do not need to be a Linux user. Ubuntu is a desktop, like Windows. The terminal work is copy the command from the page and paste it, read the output. You do not need experience navigating terminals.

5. “I am new” or “I am not technical” is not a reason to buy a hardware wallet instead. If you will not complete this README, including test spends, wait, or use a brokerage, ETF, or trust. Do not treat hardware wallets as the easy version of this guide.

## What you are setting up

A 3-of-7 air-gapped multisig using Bitcoin Core on two dedicated computers. Keys are generated on an offline machine and backed up to archival discs. Any 3 of 7 discs can spend. Up to 4 can be lost without loss of funds.

The [README](README.md) is the procedure. The [FAQ](FAQ.md) is the design rationale.
